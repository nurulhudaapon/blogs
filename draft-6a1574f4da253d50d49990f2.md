### Sikkim

![](https://cdn.hashnode.com/uploads/covers/666d4ed9e99385087973d3e2/d9ea50a5-823b-4858-bb0b-d6cfe0733642.png)

This was my first international tour, and I chose the closest destination from my country and things that I like. I like cool weather and mountains hence Sikkim was the perfect first international travel destination for me. Initially I tried searching online for a group tour or something which I can join, there weren't many. But then I thought about searching on Facebook which then led to me find group tour on Traveller of Sikkim group. I looked up the scheduled group tour, went ahead and applied for visa in the IVAC centre. Once I got my visa I booked the tour.

The tour was of 5 nights and 6 days. Since Sikkim would be very cold, I got jacket, gloves, face+head mask that would let me survive the weather.

The journey begin from a bus counter at Dhaka to Burimari, we reached their in the morning on time and then the most long and burecratic process begin first at the Bangladesh border, and then at the India border, we had been waiting in the queue whole day. Once the immigration has been completed we got a jeep towards Shiliguri, after sometime we reached Shiliguri and got a hotel temporarily to store our luggages and then some of us went out for shopping. Once the shopping is done we got a jeep again towards Gangtok, Sikkim in our Sikhism Guest House hotel, after a long journey we reached the destination, we explored the [**MG Marg Market**](https://share.google/eIg7PUpB9Xmt5kAZe) **at Gangtok.**
