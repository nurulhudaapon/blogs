Ah, that was a year to remember.

In the very January I forgot haha, need to look at my timeline to find out what I did.

Malaysia Tour:

Following my “1 international tour a year”, I had been looking for a country, and since I want to spend some time being digital nomad I was looking for some Muslim country which are developed but still peaceful, and Malaysia was one of the country on my list. But just before 10 days of my scheduled flight I got diagonized with Hepatitis A, what a surprise, my doctor prescribed me to get admitted to hospital for 12 days, I mentioned I have a flight in 10 days will it be possible to take it on? Anyway I ended up not cancelling the tour as it was a year long plan where some of my colleagues from another country booked for the same time so didn’t want to miss it and carefully took care of myself for the next 10 days to recover as much as I could and trimmed the tour down to be as restful as possible.

So as planned we flew over to Thailand where we had a layover time of 12 hours; I planned to have Thai Soup in Thailand, hence we went to one restaurant at the airport asking for Thai Soup, they are like what is "Thai Soup”, and they convinced us that every soup they have here is Thai Soup. 😂 Oops after thinking for a bit it made sense,. so we got a random soup and they were asking what do we want with soup, we said only soup, they forced us to get rice with soup, and we could only taste a spoon of it and had to discard the rest.
